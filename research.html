Research in focus

New architectures. New questions.

Our submitted work explores a molecular four-ring Brunnian link and programmable RNA assemblies with more than 100 unique components. These preprints extend the structures that can be built from nucleic acids.

Explore the preprints

Collaborative research · 2026–2028

RNA engineering for pancreatic cancer immunotherapy.

Supported by the Stand Up To Cancer–Nina Nicolai Pancreatic Cancer Innovation in Collaboration Award, our collaboration with Dr. Eric Tran at Providence Cancer Institute investigates Cap2 mRNA strategies to strengthen TCR-engineered T-cell therapy. Di Liu serves as the ASU principal investigator.
