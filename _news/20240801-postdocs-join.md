Cong Li and Zhishang Li joined the Di Liu Lab as postdoctoral researchers in 2024, contributing to the group’s growing research program in DNA and RNA engineering.

Both researchers are co-authors of the submitted manuscript on programmable self-assembly of RNA nanostructures with more than 100 unique components. Cong is also a co-first author of the submitted manuscript on a molecular four-ring Brunnian link.

**2026 update:** Zhishang completed his postdoctoral appointment and is now an Associate Professor at Ocean University of China.
