The collaboration with Dr. Eric Tran at Providence Cancer Institute received the **2026 Stand Up To Cancer–Nina Nicolai Pancreatic Cancer Innovation in Collaboration Award**.

The project, *Potentiating TCR-T Cell Therapy in Pancreatic Cancer via Interferon-resilient Cap2 mRNA Vaccine*, investigates RNA-based approaches to improve T-cell receptor-engineered T-cell therapy for pancreatic cancer. Di Liu serves as the ASU principal investigator.

The project runs from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2028. These are research strategies under investigation; the work aims to strengthen immune responses and improve therapy effectiveness.
