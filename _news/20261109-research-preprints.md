Our November 2026 research update highlights two submitted manuscripts with publicly available preprints.

## A molecular four-ring Brunnian link

In this topology, four rings are collectively linked, yet cutting any single ring releases the remaining rings.

[Read the ChemRxiv preprint](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/full/10.26434/chemrxiv.15011914/v1).

## RNA self-assembly with more than 100 unique components

This study extends the complexity of programmable RNA architectures and connects molecular assembly with structural biology.

[Read the bioRxiv preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.02.748990).

Both studies are submitted manuscripts available as preprints and have not yet been peer reviewed.
