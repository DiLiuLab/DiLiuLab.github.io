RhoFold+ combines deep learning with an RNA language model to predict three-dimensional RNA structures. The study, “Accurate RNA 3D structure prediction using a language model-based deep learning approach,” was published in *Nature Methods* 21, 2287–2298 (2024).

The work adds a computational approach to the lab’s broader effort to understand RNA structure through molecular engineering and structural biology.

[Read the article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02487-0).
