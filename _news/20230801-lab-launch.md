Di Liu joined Arizona State University in August 2023 as an Assistant Professor. The laboratory brings together nucleic acid design, nanotechnology, and structural biology to pursue three connected directions: synthetic nucleic acid topology, RNA nanostructure engineering and structural biology, and RNA-based therapeutics.
