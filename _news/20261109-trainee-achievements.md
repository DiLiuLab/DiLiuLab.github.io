Our November 2026 roundup celebrates the accomplishments of lab members during the year.

- **Cong Li:** NSF DNA32 Travel Grant and a selected oral presentation at DNA32.
- **Gengshi Wu:** NSF DNA32 Travel Grant and a selected short talk from poster submissions at DNA32.
- **Kamalesh Goggi:** Second Place Award in the Immunology category at the 33rd Annual School of Life Sciences Undergraduate Research Poster Symposium.
- **Yash Kothari:** ACS Division of Analytical Chemistry Undergraduate Award.

We also celebrate **Zhishang Li’s** transition to an Associate Professor position at Ocean University of China after his 2024–2026 postdoctoral appointment.

These achievements build on earlier trainee successes, including Olivia Holman’s SOLUR Fellowship and SMS Distinguished Biochemistry Merit Award in 2025, and Sasha Petrova’s selected oral presentation at the 5th Arizona RNA Salon Symposium.
